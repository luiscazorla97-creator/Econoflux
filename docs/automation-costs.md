# Current operating costs and limits

The [ten-minute reporting policy](reporting-automation.md) and [daily Academy calendar](crypto-academy.md) share the existing infrastructure. On 2026-11-02 the owner authorized increasing the internal AI spending ceiling from US$3 to US$6 per day. No paid service, subscription upgrade or provider billing setting was activated.

The model cap is not a publication target, minimum spend or invoice estimate. At its maximum it permits US$180 over 30 billing days or US$186 over 31; actual usage can be much lower. Unknown costs remain reserved after transport failures; completed calls settle using returned usage. Increasing the ceiling never clears existing expenditure or reservations. Failed sources consume no generation. Valid translations and successful final reviews are cached; retries do not regenerate completed phases. Daily AI accounting uses America/New_York; Academy publication days/weeks use UTC.

A normal cycle publishes one adequately sourced relevant story, exceptionally up to three, and none when evidence, novelty or budget is insufficient. Identifiable accessible journalism is eligible under the current reporting policy; primary sources are not mandatory for every ordinary report. Confirmed news, attributed rumors and leaks remain visibly distinct. Every publication must complete both languages, factual/bilingual checks, its relevant rights-recorded image and individual server-rendered SEO metadata. Existing URLs and event deduplication remain in use; missed slots do not require filler or duplicate posts.

See official [OpenAI pricing](https://openai.com/api/pricing/), [Workers limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/), [D1 limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/) and [Pages limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/) for account-dependent terms. The configured dollar cap does not include charges of an already-contracted news subscription or any Cloudflare account billing. No additional service was contracted for this implementation.
